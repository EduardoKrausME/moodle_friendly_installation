Access and users

The control center brings administrative shortcuts, account status and essential portal data closer together, reducing time spent navigating across different environments during support.

How it works

The environment page brings together shortcuts and indicators that support day-to-day operations. The name and domain are highlighted, the installed version is visible, and actions open Moodle™ software, administration, phpMyAdmin and the app area when those resources are available.

User management complements this view with search, status filters and a clear table of the accounts found. The goal is to reduce the number of steps needed to answer simple support questions.

The panel acts as a navigation and diagnostics hub without turning accounts or data from different portals into a single installation.

The selected domain remains the context, including when the control center displays users and administrative shortcuts.
